## 1. Solar PV installation & maintenance (SOL)

| Element | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Concept** | A licensed installer in Accra selling **standard hybrid packages**: 3 kVA starter and 5 kVA / 5 kWh systems, installed with warranty, plus paid maintenance visits. |
| **Value proposition** | "Certified, warrantied backup power at a mid-market price — with financing." Below established installers (GHS 55–85k for 5 kVA, ENE-44), far more accountable than marketplace kits (ENE-18). |
| **Launch segment** | SMEs losing sales in outages (pharmacies, cold-drink sellers, salons, printers, clinics), then middle-income homes. See [market-demand.md §3](../research/market-demand.md#3-customer-segmentation). |
| **Revenue model** | Installed system price (ex-VAT, VAT-registered company). Customers pay a deposit of at least 70% before equipment is purchased and the balance on commissioning. Maintenance visits at GHS 600. Financing is arranged through bank partners (Ecobank ASAP, Stanbic; ENE-46, ENE-47), not provided by the business. |
| **Operations** | 1 certified lead technician + 1 assistant + 1 sales/admin officer + owner-manager; showroom with a live demonstration system; motor tricycle for equipment. Capacity ~8–10 installations per month per crew. |
| **Licences** | Company registration; VAT and SSNIT; Energy Commission electrical-wiring certification (REG-23) and renewable-energy installation licence (fee not published); assembly business permit. Buy only Energy Commission-registered products (REG-24). |
| **KPIs** | Site surveys per week; survey-to-deposit conversion (target ≥ 30%); systems installed per month (break-even ≈ 2.4); gross margin per system (≥ 30%); warranty call-backs (< 5%). |
| **Foreign-investor note** | Selling imported equipment may be classified as **trading** under Act 1173 (US$500k cash equity, and it applies even to a minority foreign beneficial owner, s.35(3); REG-05). A foreign investor should obtain a legal opinion, or structure the business as an installation-services company where customers buy equipment from licensed distributors. |

## 2. Laundry & dry-cleaning with pickup and delivery (LAU)

| Element | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Concept** | A neighbourhood processing shop in the Madina–Adenta–East Legon corridor offering per-kg wash & fold, pressed garments and special items (suits, duvets, traditional wear), with free scheduled pickup by motorbike. |
| **Value proposition** | "Guaranteed 48-hour turnaround, every item tagged and photographed." This targets the reliability and garment-damage complaints cited in the market (SVC-13). |
| **Launch segment** | Working professionals and young families; then hostels and guest houses on bulk contracts. |
| **Revenue model** | GHS 18/kg wash & fold; GHS 10 per pressed garment; GHS 40 per special item (SVC-32–35); monthly subscriptions; B2B contracts. |
| **Operations** | 3 attendants + 1 rider + owner-manager; 2 × 20 kg washers, 1 washer-dryer plus line drying, industrial steam iron, 5.5 kVA generator, 2,000 L water storage. |
| **Licences** | Business-name registration; assembly business operating permit; EPA registration for wastewater (REG-25, REG-26). |
| **KPIs** | Active customers; kg processed per day (break-even ≈ 40 kg + garments); turnaround compliance (≥ 95% within 48 h); damage claims (< 0.5% of items); subscription share of revenue. |
| **Foreign-investor note** | A non-trading service not on the reserved list, so eligible under Act 1173 with **no minimum capital**. The GIPA fee (US$3,500 as a JV) keeps the total at about US$19,200 (REG-08). |

## 3. Broiler production with direct off-take (BRO)

| Element | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Concept** | Two open-sided houses on a leased peri-urban acre, with staggered placements of ~1,100 chicks per month, selling ~900 birds per month live or dressed directly to chop bars, caterers and festive buyers. |
| **Value proposition** | "Fresh, consistent local chicken delivered to your kitchen." Freshness and reliability, because frozen imports win on price (AGR-12, AGR-33). |
| **Launch segment** | Chop bars and local restaurants (weekly contracts); festive pre-orders. |
| **Revenue model** | Blended GHS 105 per bird (live and dressed); manure sales. |
| **Operations** | 2 farm hands + farm manager; brooders, feeders, drinkers; on-farm dressing kit and freezers; veterinary retainer; strict biosecurity (all-in/all-out). |
| **Licences** | Company registration; district assembly permit; veterinary services registration; EPA screening; FDA requirements if dressed birds are sold to retail (SVC-21). |
| **KPIs** | Mortality (≤ 8%); feed conversion ratio (≤ 2.25); average sale weight; **price realised per bird (≥ GHS 105)**; share of each batch pre-sold before placement (≥ 80%). |
| **Foreign-investor note** | Agriculture is open with no minimum capital under Act 1173. The GIPA fee pushes the total to about US$21,300 as a JV, above the US$20k ceiling. |

## 4. Last-mile delivery for online sellers (DEL)

| Element | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Concept** | A 4-bike, licensed courier fleet in Accra running **scheduled same-day routes** for repeat online sellers, pharmacies and bakeries, with same-day MoMo remittance of cash-on-delivery collections. |
| **Value proposition** | "Your deliveries done on schedule — and your COD money in your MoMo wallet the same day." This targets the cash-handling and reliability problems sellers face with ad-hoc riders and apps (ENE-51, ENE-53). |
| **Launch segment** | Instagram/TikTok/WhatsApp sellers with 5–30 orders a day. |
| **Revenue model** | GHS 25 per drop (B2B contract rate) + GHS 4 COD settlement fee; monthly contracts with minimum volumes. |
| **Operations** | 4 riders on base pay plus per-drop incentive; 1 dispatcher; owner-manager; GPS trackers; dispatch software; insured bikes. |
| **Licences** | Business-name registration; DVLA commercial registration; rider licences and **union/company membership** ("No Union, No Okada", Nov 2026); courier rider registration GHS 570 per rider (ENE-32–34). |
| **KPIs** | Drops per bike per day (break-even ≈ 11); on-time rate (≥ 95%); COD remitted within 24 h (100%); active contracted sellers; rider retention. |
| **Foreign-investor note** | Goods delivery is not on the reserved list (the reservation covers taxi/car hire with fewer than 25 vehicles). Eligible; the total is about US$20,000 as a JV, right at the ceiling. |

## 5. SME digital-commerce enablement (DIG)

| Element | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Concept** | A 3-person service business that takes micro and small firms "online and paid": a WhatsApp Business catalogue, MoMo checkout and payment links, a Google profile and a landing page (one-off set-up), then monthly social-commerce management, plus paid seller-training workshops. |
| **Value proposition** | "Start selling and getting paid online this week — for less than an agency." Priced below agency entry tiers (GHS 1,500–2,500/month, ENE-57, ENE-58). |
| **Launch segment** | Restaurants and food vendors, then boutiques and beauty businesses. |
| **Revenue model** | GHS 2,500 set-up; GHS 1,200 monthly retainer; GHS 300 per workshop attendee. |
| **Operations** | Owner-manager + digital marketer + content creator; co-working desks; prepaid software licences. |
| **Licences** | Business-name registration; assembly permit; Data Protection Commission registration. |
| **KPIs** | Paying retainer clients (break-even ≈ 10–11); monthly churn (< 5%); set-ups per month; client sales uplift (case studies). |
| **Foreign-investor note** | Eligible; capital-light. Even as a wholly foreign company (GIPA fee US$5,250) the total is about US$13,800. **This is the most accessible option for a foreign investor**, but also the least proven. |
