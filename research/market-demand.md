# Market Demand, Market Sizing and Customer Segmentation

[← Back to README](../README.md) · Related: [industry-analysis.md](industry-analysis.md) · [shortlisted-businesses.md](../opportunities/shortlisted-businesses.md) · [competitor-analysis.md](../strategic-analysis/competitor-analysis.md)

> **Purpose:** Validate demand for the five shortlisted businesses and size each market (TAM → SAM → SOM).
>
> **Important:** No official statistics exist for these small-business segments in Ghana. Every market size below is an **[E] estimate**: the arithmetic is shown, each input is labelled [F] or [A], and an independent cross-check is used where one exists. Treat the figures as **orders of magnitude**, not precise values. The value of the exercise is to show whether the planned SOM is a plausible share of the market, i.e. whether the base-case volumes are realistic.

**Definitions used here:** **TAM** = annual spending by all plausible buyers in the stated geography. **SAM** = the part this business model can serve (its geography, price tier and channel). **SOM** = the share captured in the base-case financial model.

## 2. Market sizing

### 2.1 Solar installation — residential and SME hybrid systems

| Step | Calculation | Result | Label |
|---|---|---|---|
| Population of Greater Accra + Ashanti (2021 census) | 5.46m + 5.44m | 10.9m | [F] MAC-14 (secondary reporting of 2021 PHC) |
| Households | ÷ 3.6 persons per household | 3.03m | [A] household size |
| Households able to buy (cash or 7-year bank loan at 15%) | × 15% | 454k | [A] |
| Power-dependent SMEs able to buy | 1.87m establishments × 40% in the two regions × 10% | 75k | [F] MAC-20; [A] shares |
| **Potential buyers** | 454k + 75k | **~529k** | [E] |
| Installed value of that potential | × US$3,300 average system (base-case mix) | ~US$1.75bn | [E] |
| **TAM (annualised over a 10-year adoption horizon)** | ÷ 10 | **~US$175m per year** | [E] |
| **SAM** — mid-market hybrid systems in Greater Accra (excludes C&I and PAYG) | × 50% Greater Accra × 60% mid-market | **~US$53m per year** | [E] |
| Cross-check: national distributed-solar target | 219 MW new distributed + small PV, 2026–2030 (ENE-13) ≈ 44 MW/yr × US$0.9–1.1 per watt | US$40–48m per year nationally | [E] |
| **SOM (base case, year 1)** | US$151.6k year-1 revenue ÷ SAM | **≈ 0.3% of SAM** (~45 systems) | [P] |

**Interpretation:** The planned volume is a small share of a market that two independent approaches put at roughly US$40–55m a year. The base case does not depend on winning a large market share; it depends on **converting enough leads at a mid-market price**. Reaching 1% of SAM (~US$0.5m revenue) would need ~13 systems per month, i.e. two to three installation crews.

### 2.2 Laundry service — outsourced laundry in Greater Accra

| Step | Calculation | Result | Label |
|---|---|---|---|
| Greater Accra households | 5.46m ÷ 3.6 | 1.52m | [F] MAC-14; [A] household size |
| Households paying for laundry at least monthly | × 20% | 303k | [A] |
| Annual spend per user household | GHS 150 per month × 12 (≈ 0.75 of a FUA "regular basket", SVC-34) | GHS 1,800 | [A] |
| **TAM** | 303k × GHS 1,800 ÷ 11.70 | **~US$47m per year** | [E] |
| **SAM** — one shop's 5 km pickup catchment (e.g., Madina–Adenta–East Legon corridor, ~250k residents) | 250k ÷ 3.6 × 20% × GHS 1,800 ÷ 11.70 | **~US$2.1m per year** | [A] catchment; [E] |
| **SOM (base case)** | US$34k year-1 revenue; US$44.6k month-12 annualised | **≈ 1.6–2.1% of SAM** | [P] |

**Interpretation:** About 2% of a neighbourhood catchment is a realistic target for one well-run shop, but the TAM rests on two assumptions (20% usage, GHS 150/month) that should be tested first in a 100-household survey.

### 2.5 Broiler farm — fresh local chicken in Greater Accra

| Step | Calculation | Result | Label |
|---|---|---|---|
| National poultry-meat consumption | 270k t imported + 60k t local | ~330k t | [F] AGR-03 (as cited) |
| **TAM (national, at frozen-import price)** | 330k t × GHS 33.40/kg ÷ 11.70 | **~US$940m per year** | [F] AGR-12 price; [E] |
| **SAM** — local fresh chicken in Greater Accra | 60k t × 17.7% population share × GHS 49/kg ÷ 11.70 | **~US$44m per year** | [F] AGR-33 price; [E] |
| **SOM (base case)** | Steady state 900 birds/month × 12 × ~2 kg (year 1: ~9,000 birds) | ~21.6 t ≈ **0.2% of SAM**; US$82k year-1 revenue | [P] |

**Interpretation:** The TAM is the largest of the five, but almost all of it is **imported frozen chicken at a price local producers cannot match**. The relevant SAM is the fresh-local segment, which is far smaller. Even there, 2026 shows that supply can outrun buyers (AGR-06, AGR-36).

## 3. Customer segmentation

Segments are prioritised on three tests: **urgency of need**, **ability to pay** and **reachability through low-cost channels**.

### Solar installation

| Segment | Need | Ability to pay | Channel | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **SMEs losing stock or sales in outages** (pharmacies, cold-drink sellers, salons, print shops, clinics) | Continuity of trade; replace petrol generator | Medium–high; can justify on lost-sales avoided | Direct sales walks; trade associations; WhatsApp | **1 — launch segment** |
| **Middle-income homeowners** (remote workers, families with children) | Comfort, security, appliance protection | Medium; often need instalments | Referrals, Facebook/TikTok, bank-loan partners | **2** |
| Schools, churches, small hotels | Lighting, pumps, refrigeration | Medium; slow procurement | Direct; programme-funded tenders (ENE-14) | 3 |
| Low-income households | Basic lighting | Low | PAYG providers (Bboxx) already serve this | Not targeted |

|---|---|---|---|---|

| **Private hostels and student residences** | Bulk weekly service | Medium (price-sensitive, high volume) | Hostel managers; bulk packages (SVC-33) | **2** |
| Guest houses, Airbnb hosts, small hotels | Linen turnaround | Medium | B2B contracts | 3 |

## 4. Demand risks to validate first (before investing)

| Business | Hypothesis to test | Cheapest test (2–4 weeks) | Pass mark |
|---|---|---|---|
| Solar installation | SMEs will pay a 30–70% deposit for a mid-market system | 30 SME interviews + 10 free site surveys | ≥ 3 paid deposits |
| Laundry service | ≥ 20% of target households pay for laundry monthly | 100-household survey + 2-week pop-up pickup trial | ≥ 60 paying trial customers |
| Broiler farm | Buyers will commit to GHS 105 per bird | Pre-sell one batch to chop bars | 80% of a batch pre-sold |
| Delivery fleet | Sellers will sign monthly contracts | Recruit anchor sellers with 2 rented bikes | ≥ 10 sellers at ≥ 15 drops/bike/day |
| Digital services | Micro-firms pay GHS 1,000–1,200 monthly | Sell 5 pilot retainers | ≥ 5 paying clients by week 6 |
