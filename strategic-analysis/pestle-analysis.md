### Conclusions from PESTLE

1. **Solar (+15) and digital services (+12) have the strongest macro tailwinds**, with delivery (+8) next. For solar the main offset is FX exposure, which is managed through pricing policy (section 6 of [shortlisted-businesses.md](../opportunities/shortlisted-businesses.md)).
2. **Broiler production has a net-negative external environment (−3).** Strong import-substitution demand is outweighed by disease, feed-price, subsidised-competition and food-safety enforcement exposures. This does not make it unattractive, but it does make it the **highest-risk** option at small scale. That is reflected in the [risk register](risk-assessment.md).
3. **Laundry's environment is only mildly positive (+2), and its main external exposure is utilities** (power and water), so backup power and water storage are non-negotiable items in its startup budget.
